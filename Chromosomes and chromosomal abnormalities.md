Pathologies
- Down syndrome - trisomy 21 (3 of chromosome 21)
- Edward syndrome - trisomy 18
- Patau syndrome - trisomy 13 
- Turner syndrome - female missing 1 X chromosome
	- X chromosome aneuploidy is better tolerated due to X chromosome inactivation #link 
- Others are not compatible with life
