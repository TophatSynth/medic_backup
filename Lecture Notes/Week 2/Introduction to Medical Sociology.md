## History
- Shifted from a more religious/superstitious model to a biomedical model (enlightenment and science)
- Then to a biopsychosocial model (Holistic)

## Key concepts
- Social determinant of health
	- Impact of social factors on an individuals' health and wellbeing. 
- Health disparities/inequalities
	- Differences in healthcare access and health status across society
- Health and illness behaviour
	- How people perceive, experience and respond to health and illness. Often influenced by cultural, social and psychological factors
- Social construction of illness
	- Society defines and interprets what is considered "illness" and "health". Eg menopause and pathologisation
- Public health and epidemiology
- Chronic illness and disability
