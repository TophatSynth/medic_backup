Muscle types 
- Pennate 
	- Fibres are at an angle to the line of force, usually insert into a central tendon
	- Unipennate - all the muscle fibres are on one side of the tendon
	- Bipennate - muscle fibres are on two sides of the tendon
	- Multipennate - muscle fibres attach on multiple sides
- Fusiform
	- Spindle shaped 
- Convergent
	- Broad attachment -> narrow attachment, eg trapezius
- Quadrate 
	- Square shape 
- Circular
	- Ring shaped
- Multiheaded 
	- Multiple distinct points of origin

Aponeurosis 
- Flat sheet of tendon - the white stuff in the back between muscles

Classifications of types of bones
- Long - Longer than it is wide, cylindrical - eg metacarpals, phalanges
- Short - Cube-like - eg carpals, tarsals
- Flat - Thin, flat, often curved - eg scapula, skull
- Sesamoid - small, round, encapsulated in tendon - eg patella
- Irregular - Doesn't fit any other classification - eg vertebrae
