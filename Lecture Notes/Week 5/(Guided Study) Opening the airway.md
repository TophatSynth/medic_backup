	- Lips, mouth, tongue, outer nose, nasal cavity, septum, paranasal sinuses, soft and hard palate

3. Jaw thrust, Chin lift/head tilt
	- a. When lifting the chin what bone are you moving? 
		- The mandible
	- b. Where should your fingers be when performing jaw thrust? 
		- The notch in the mandible just under the ear
	- c. What is the predominant primary tissue type comprising the tongue? 
		- Muscle
	- d. What property of this tissue type is lost in the unconscious patient? 
		- Tone
	- e. What bone is your left hand placed over in the head tilt chin lift manoeuvre if you are placed at the patient’s / casualty’s right side? 
		- Frontal bone
	- f. What structural design allows the trachea to maintain an open passage? 
		- C-shaped rings of cartilage

		- Temporomandibular joint
