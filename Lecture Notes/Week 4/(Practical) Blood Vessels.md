#anki 
Development of atherosclerosis
- Damage to the endothelium via smoking, toxins etc -> endothelium is broken up, allowing LDL  cholesterol to enter the tunica intima. LDL is also modified due to the same chemicals. 
- Macrophages in the tunica intima usually take up LDL via LDL receptors, with a feedback loop that prevents more LDL uptake once there is enough within the macrophage
- The modification to the LDL causes the feedback loop to not work -> excessive accumulation of LDL within the macrophage -> unable to autophage all the lipids due to sheer volume -> become "foam cells" 
- Foam cells rupture -> lysing LDL under the endothelium - LDL core. Smooth muscle cells proliferate around the core and secrete collagen to try and seal it off - fibrous cap
- More foam cells lyse -> growth of the atheromatous plaque 
- Increased resistance to blood flow, ischaemia! Yay!
