## Clavicle ![[Pasted image 20260126120921.png]]
- Structure
	- S-shaped
	- Smooth superior surface, rough inferior surface 
	- Round proximal end, flat + hooked distal end
- Fractured 
	- Usually - middle, proximal end goes up, distal end goes down 
	- Most commonly fractured bone 
		- Takes all the force from UL, and sternoclavicular joint is _very_ strong
- Sternoclavicular joint
	- Very strong ligaments around joint - synovial lined with fibrocartilage
	- Has an articular disc
- Acromioclavicular joint - joins with clavicle ![[Pasted image 20260126164317.png]]![[Pasted image 20260126121514.png]]
	- Acromioclavicular ligament, conoid/trapezoid ligament (coracoclavicular ligament)

## Hand
- 27 bones in total
- Carpal 
	- Proximal and distal rows 
	- SLTP, TTCH
	- Proximal Row
		- S – Scaphoid
		- L – Lunate 
		- T – Triquetrum
		- (P – Pisiform) <- sesamoid
	- Distal Row
		- Tz – Trapezium
		- Td – Trapezoid 
		- C – Capitate 
		- H – Hammate
	- Radiocarpal joint 
		- Only radius articulates with the wrist, ulna separated by fibrocartilage disk
		- There are pal./dor. radio-carpal ligaments, pal. ulno-carpal ligament, and radial/ulnar collateral ligaments
		- 
	- Carpal joints
		- Intercarpal joints, mid-carpal joints (between rows) - gliding synovial joints
		- Pisoiotriquetral joint - joint bt pisiform and triquetrum 
		- Carpal-metcarpal joints (CMCJ's) - all condyloid except first CMCJ (thumb)
- Carpal tunnel
	- Carpal arch formed by the proximal row of carpal bones 
	- Forms the carpal tunnel when closed off with the [[Introduction to the limbs|flexor retinaculum]], which connects the hamate and pisiform on one side to the trapezium and scaphoid on the other ![[Pasted image 20260127103300.png]]
- Other joints in the hand
	- Metacarpal-phalangeal joint - condylar joint, has collateral and palmar ligaments
	- Interphalangeal joints - hinge joints, has collateral and palmar ligaments
- Movement
	- Middle finger only abducts 
	- Everything else is relative to middle finger

## Arteries
- Starts from brachiocephalic trunk/arch of aorta -> subclavian arteries (ends at lateral border of 1st rib) -> axillary artery (ends at inferior border of Teres major) -> splits off at 3 parts ![[Pasted image 20260126124516.png]]
	- 1st before Pec. minor
		- Superior thoracic artery - supplies intercostal spaces of ribs 1 and 2
	- 2nd behind Pec minor
		- Thoraco-acromial artery - "pokes up behind pec minor and goes blegh :P" 
		- Lateral thoracic artery - Moves down the lateral thorax along the mid-axillary line #tofinish, supplies the lateral and anterior thoracic wall + lateral aspect of the breast
	- 3rd after Pec. minor
		- Subscapular artery - "chonky", branches -> thoraco-dorsal artery (supplies the post. thoracic wall), circumflex scapular artery (goes around the scapula)
		- Ant./Post. circumflex humeral artery - go around the humerus, form an anastamosis 
	- "Screw The Lawyer, Save A Pigeon" 
- 
- Scapular 
	- Scapular anastamosis 
	- Dorsal scapular and suprascapular arteries - go over the top of the scapular and anastomosise with the circumflex scapular artery (from the subscapular artery) - bypass from subclavian to 3rd part of axillary artery
- Brachial artery
	- Ends when it bifurcates anterior to the elbow (exact location variable bt ppl) 
	- 3 branches
		- Profunda Barachii (profound - deep)
		- Superior Ulnar collateral
		- Inferior Ulnar Collateral
- Radial and ulnar arteries 
	- Ulnar larger/deeper than radial
	- Forms anastomosis 
	- Anterior/posterior?? #tofinish 
	- Anatomical snuffbox :D Druggssssssss
- Interosseous arteries
	- Branches deep off the ulnar artery -> Anterior interosseous + Posterior interosseous
- Wrist 
	- Neither radial/ulnar enter carpal tunnel - radial passes posteriorly to the carpal bones, ulnar poasses anteriorly (superficially) 
	- Allen's Test - testing for flow through the anastamoses 
	- Each one gives a palmar and dorsal carpal branch (4 in total), connect up to form dorsal/palmar carpal arches 
- Scaphoid fractures 
	- Easily fractured 
	- Scaphoid gets blood supply from distal end - if fractured, can cause necrosis #tofinish
