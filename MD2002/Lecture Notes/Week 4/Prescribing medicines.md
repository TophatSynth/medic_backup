MHRA - regulatory body for meds
Marketing authorisation - product license

Off-label - has a license but being used outside of the license specs

Misuse of drugs act 1971 - penalties for manufacture, supply, or possession of a drug 

Misuse of drugs regulations 2001 - regulates all that, + prescribing, import/export etc 
- Cannot prescribe more than a set amount of a certain drug at a time

Human medicines regulations 2012 - consolidated modernised rules in line with EU regulations
3 legal categories of medicines
- GSL - general sales list
- P - pharmacy only
- POM - prescription only medicine
