Atherosclerosis contributes to half of all deaths in the western world 

# Risk factors
- Age
- Sex (post menopausal women)
- Genetics
- Hyperlipidaemia 
- Hypertension
- Smoking 
- DM

# Pathogenesis 
- ![[Pasted image 20260218102351.png]]
- [[(Practical) Blood Vessels]]
- Chronic endothelial injury/dysfunction 
	- Causes increased endothelial permeability -> monocytes migrate into blood vessel walls 
- Hyperlipidaemia 
	- Impairs endothelial function -> accumulates within tunica intima -> oxidative modification of LDL 
	- Modified LDL ingested by macrophages -> foam cells 
		- Inhibits motility of macrophages
		- Macrophages release cytokines - chemotactic for more macrophages and cytotoxic to endothelial and smooth muscle cells 
			- Eg IL1, TNF, growth factors, interferons, etc
		- Forms fatty streaks on inner surface of blood vessel ![[Pasted image 20260218102144.png]]![[Pasted image 20260218102523.png]]
			- Matures into fibro-fatty atheroma as smooth muscle cells proliferate and deposit collage/ECM

# Morphology
- Atheromatous plaque
- Patchy and raised white/yellow area 
- 0.3 - 1.5cm in height 
- Core of lipid, fibrous cap
- Complications
	- Calcification
	- Rupture or ulceration -> haemorrhage
	- Thrombosis
	- Aneurysmal dilation aneurysm 
- Fatty streaks can develop even in babies
	- _May_ be precursors of plaques, not necessarily  tho
	- Takes a few decades to develop into smth clinical - will only find out if complications happen 

# Prevention
- Primary
	- Stop smoking!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
	- Control hypertension and weight 
	- Lowering total LDL
- Secondary
	- Prevent complications (no shit sherlock....)
	- Antiplatelet drugs in thrombosis
	- Lower blood lipid levels
