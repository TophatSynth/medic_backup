# LMN Lesion
- Wasting 
- Fasciculations as nerve attempts to regenerate locally
- Reduced tone
- Reduced/absent reflexes


# UMN Lesion
- No wasting in the initial stages
- Increased tone (aka spasticity) 
- Clonus - repeated contraction when muscle put under tension
- Weakness - quadriplegic/paraplegic/hemiplegic
