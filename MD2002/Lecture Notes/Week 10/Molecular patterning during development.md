Limb development
- Hox genes determine intial body axis and position of limb along that axis
	- Actual development is determined by other genes (eg TBX5 for upper limb, TBX4 for lower limb)
- Limb growth is regulated along proximo-distal/antero-posterior/dorso-ventral axes
	- Proximo-distal 
		- Controlled by apical ectodermal ridge (AER) ![[Pasted image 20260413114040.png]]
		- Induces underlying tissue to remain undifferentiated and rapidly proliferating (progress zone) - as cell move away from AER -> differentiate into cartilage and muscle 
		- ![[Pasted image 20260413114338.png]]
	- Anteroposterior axis
		- Regulated by Zone of polarising activity (ZPA)
		- ![[Pasted image 20260413114615.png]]
	- Dorsoventral axis
		- BMP's #link

Pathologies 
- Amelia - complete absence of limbs
- Meromelia - partial absence of limbs
- Phocomelia - absence of long bones
- Micromelia - segments are abnormally short 
- Limb defects often associated with other abnormalities affects CVS/GU and craniofacial structures
