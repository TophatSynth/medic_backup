#anki 

4 types of chemotherapy agents
- Alkylating agents
	- Most common 
	- Forms covalent bonds with nucleophillic substances in the cell - causes intrastrand crosslinking of DNA
		- Guanine usually H bonds with cytosine 
		- When the 7 Nitrogen of guanine is alkylated it becoms more acidic -> can mispair with thymine during DNA synthesis -> mutation -> hopefully cell dies 
		- Also destabilises guanine -> more opportunity for mutation 
	- Major groups 
		- Nitrogen mustards
			- Eg cyclophosphamide
			- Activated in liver to active form 
		- Ethylenimines
		- Alkylsulphonates 
			- Eg busulphan 
			- Selective effect on bone marrow - depresses formation of granulocytes/platelets/RBC's 
		- Hydrazines and triazines
		- Nitrosoureas 
			- Eg lomustine, carmustine 
			- Lipid soluble - can cross blood brain barrier -> treat tumours of brain/meninges
		- Platinum based compounds 
			- Eg cisplatin 
			- Has Cl inside - enters the cell, Cl dissciates, leaving a reactive complex that reacts with water and interacts with DNA 
- Antimetabolites
	- Antifolates - ┐(ツ)┌ fuck if I know
	- Antipyrimidines - inhibits DNA polymerase #link 
	- Antipurines - also inhibits DNA polymerase
- Cytotoxic antibiotics 
	- Prevent cell division, usually by direct action on DNA 
	- Anthracyclines - binds to DNA, inhibits DNA and RNA synthesis 
	- Dactinomycin - prevents transcription 
	- Bleomycins - degrades DNA -> release of free bases and chain fragmentation 
	- Mitomycin - crosslinks DNA, also generates free radicals to degrade DNA
- Plant derivates
	- Spindle poisons - affects microtubule function/prevents spindle formation 
	- Topo I/II inhbitors 
- Other
	- Hormone inhibitors
	- Monoclonal antibodies
	- Protein kinase inhibitors 
	- Misc


Drawbacks
- Targets cell proliferation only - lots of side effects in cells with high turnover
- Non specific 
- Tumour lysis syndrome
	- Acute and rapid cell lysis -> large amounts of cell metabolites in blood 
		- Hyperuricaemia, hyperphosphataemia, hyperkalaemia, hypocalcaemia 
	- Emergency - acute renal failure, cardiac arrest, death. 
	- Have to risk assess patient before chemo, and monitor during treatment
