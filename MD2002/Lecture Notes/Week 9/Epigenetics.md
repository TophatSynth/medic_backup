Epigenetics - stuff that adds onto genes
- Eg DNA methylation 
	- Heritable modification of DNA that doesn't alter the primary sequence of DNA -> altered gene expression
	- Usually leads to gene silencing - inhibits gene transcription
- Can change over course of lifetime

Defects in DNA methylation machinery
- Prader-WIlli syndrome - learning disabilities/obesity
- Angelman syndrome - learning disabilities/jerky movements/inappropriate laughter

Hetero/euchromatin
- Can de/condense genes to in/activate them 

X chromosome inactivation 
- Important in X chromosome linked pathologies
