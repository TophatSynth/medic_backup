# Septic arthritis
- Presentation 
	- Hot swollen joints
	- Usually 1-2 week history of red/painful/restricted joint, with fever, sweats, rigors 
	- Medical emergency - can lead to irreversible joint damage - case fatality is ~11-50%
	- Usually large joints are affected - knee, hip, lumbosacral 
	- More common in elderly people/very young children, but can affect anyone 
- If they have preexisting arthritis, the joint will sow signs out of proportions to the disease 
- Causes
	- Low virulence pathogens/fungi can delay presentation 
	- Most will be staph aureus, strep pyogenes, mycobacterium tuberculosis 
	- Pasturella from animal bites 
- Newman definition of septic arthitis - one of four points must be met
	1. Isolation of pathogen from affected joint
	2. Isolation of pathogen from another source in the context of a hot red joint suspicious of sepsis
	3. Typical clinical faetures and turbid joint fluid in the presence of previous antibiotic treatment 
	4. Postmortem or patological features suspicious of septic arthritis
- Predisposing conditions 
	- Rheumatoid arthritis (immunocompromised) or osteoarthritis 
	- Joint prosthesis - medication cannot penetrate joint
	- Intravenous drug abuse - needle sharing
	- Alcohol excess
	- Diabetes - systemic immunocompromise
	- Previous intra-articular corticosteroid injection - corticosteriods impede immune system
	- Cutaneous ulcers

# Reactive arthritis 
- Presentation 
	- Secondary inflammatory reaction in a joint to an infection elsewhere in the body
- Aetiology 
	- Main - chlamydia 
	- Others, but like.... too many ![[Pasted image 20260402151954.png]]
- Management
	- Treated with full dose NSAID's and treatment of the main cause


# Osteomyelitis
- Presentation
	- Inflammation of the bone/marrow usually caused by pyogenic bacteria, rarely by mycobacteria/fungi 
	- Mostly in femur/tibia
	- Painful swollen site, fever, reduced movement (only sign in very young patient), paraplegia (rare)
- Aetiology
	- Can spread from septic arthritis 
	- Haematogenous spread
		- Usually asymptomatic, skin sepsis may be present
		- Organisms will setlle in the growing metaphysis near growth plate/epiphysis, bc most blood supply #link ![[Pasted image 20260402152225.png]]
	- Compound fracture from trauma 
	- Foreign body in bone
		- Shrapnel etc
		- Orthopedic implants 
	- Organisms 
		- Mostly staph aureus 
		- Different predisposing conditions are risk factors for infection by different organisms ![[Pasted image 20260402152602.png]]
- Diagnosis
	- Blood cultures
	- Samples
		- Pus
		- WBC
		- CRP
	- X rays 
	- MRI's 
- Treatment
	- Antibiotics
	- Drainage/removal of affected bone

# Prosthetic joint infections (PJI)
- Lots of incidents yearly 
- Costs healthcare sector a lot
- Infection -> poor survival outcomes ![[Pasted image 20260402153930.png]]
- Presentation
	- Joint pain, fever, swelling, wound
- Aetiology
	- Risk factors - similar to septic arthritis
	- Microbes - similar to septic arthritis 
- Diagnosis
	- Plain radiograph
- Treatment
	- Surgery - joint fusion/amputation
	- Antibiotics
