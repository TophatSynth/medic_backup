# Definitions ![[Pasted image 20260330111126.png]]
- Hyperplasia - increased division of cels 
- Hypertrophy - increased growth of size of cells 
- Genes
	- Males have XY chromosomes
	- Females have XX chromosomes

# Pathologies of growth
- Turner's syndrome
	- Female with partial or complete absence of an X chromosome
	- Born smaller and grow up shorter, underdeveloped ovaries
	- SHOX gene - expressed in chondrocytes of [[B O N E S#Growth of bone|growth plate]] 
- Down syndrome #tofinish 
- Beckwith-Wiedemann Syndrome
	- 2 chromosomes from one parent and none from other 
	- Overgrowth in early childhood
- Pituitary gigantism
	- Increased growth hormone, often from pituitary tumours 
	- Gigantism (woah)
- Achondroplasia
	- Dwarfism
	- Autosomal dominant
	- Affects FGFR3 ![[Pasted image 20260330113640.png]]
		- Activates RTK receptors -> signal -> limits growth - ligand gets unbound after a while 
		- Mutation - receptor is bound for much longer -> larger signal for longer
			- OR loss of FGFR3 causes increased growth


# Pathologies of differentiation
- Metaplasia - change of differentiated cell type, due to altered cellular env
	- Often epithelial/mesenchymal cells - eg lung in smokers
- Dysplasia - increased cell proliferation (basically tumours) - can often progress to malignancy 
- Neoplasia - abnormal uncoordinated excessive cell proliferation 
- Agenesis - failure to develop organ/structure
- Atresia - failure to develop a lumen 
- Hypoplasia - failure of organ to develop to normal size
- Ectopia/heterotopia - abnormal position of an organ/part of organ
