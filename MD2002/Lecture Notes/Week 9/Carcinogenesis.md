Causes of cancer 
1. Chemicals 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260410124921.png]]
	- Aromatic amines - metabolised in the liver to a carcinogenic metabolite, then detoxified into glucuronide and excreted by kidneys - however, the urothelial cells express β-glucuronidase -> makes glucuronide into a carcinogen again
- Cigarette smoking
	- Gets metabolised -> binds to DNA
- Carcinogens in diet
	- Nitrites/nitrates - gut bacteria convert them into carcinogens 
	- GI tract cancers - mostly in large intestine - increased expression of Bcl2 - gene that protects damaged colon cells from dying -> survive and accumulate mutations -> cancer 
- UV light carcinogenesis
	- Malignant melanoma 
	- Xeroderma pigmentosum - rare autosomal recessive disease - inabilty to repair skin damage from UV -> very easily forms cancer
- Radation carcinogenesis
