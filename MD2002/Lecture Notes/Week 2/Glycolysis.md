# 10 step pathway
- Happens in cytoplasm
- ![[Pasted image 20260204101756.png]]![[Pasted image 20260204102326.png]]
- 1 glucose -> 2 pyruvate -> Acetyl-CoA 
- 2 ATP needed at first, 4 ATP generated later -> net 2 ATP per glucose
- 3 stages
	- Investment 
	- Cleavage 
	- Energy harvest
- Strategy:
	- Add phosphoryl groups to glucose and intermediates
	- Chemically convert phosphorylated intermediates to compounds with high energy phosphate bond potential
	- Chemically couple the hydrolysis of reactive compounds to generate ATP (and NADH+ + H+)
- Steps
	1. Phosphorylation of glucose at C6 ![[Pasted image 20260204103410.png]]
		- Requires ATP - increases energy of glucose and locks it inside the cell 
	2. Conversion of glucose-6-P to fructose-6-P  ![[Pasted image 20260204103415.png]]
		- Ring opens to enable isomerisation -> F-6-P
	3. F-6-P phosphorylated at C1 -> FBP (Fructose 1,6-bisphosphate) ![[Pasted image 20260204103423.png]]
		- V unstable 
		- Done by phosphofructokinase (PFK) - [[Introduction to Enzymes]] 
	4. Aldolase cleaves FBP into 2 trioses (3C sugar) ![[Pasted image 20260204103434.png]]
		- Generates both Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (GAP) and Dihydroxyacetone phosphate (DHAP)
	5. GAP -> DHAP by isomerase
	6. Oxidation and phsophorylation of GAP by NAD+ and Pi ![[Pasted image 20260204103445.png]]
		- High energy intermediate 
	7. First formation of ATP <- dephosphorylated GAP ![[Pasted image 20260204103452.png]]
		- Synthesises 2ATP and 3-phosphoglycerate (3PG)
	8. 3PG converted to 2PG ![[Pasted image 20260204103502.png]]
	9. 2PG dehydrated -> Phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP)
		- Converst low energy phosphate bond into high energy bond 
	10. Hydrolysis of PEP -> pyruvate
		- Generates another 2 ATP
		- Irreversible reaction 
- What happens to pyruvate after
	- Aerobic - converted to Acetyl-CoA -> Krebbs cycle
	- Anaerobic - converted to lactate 
		- W/o oxygen, NAD+ can't be replenished 
		- Pyruvate converted to lactate to regenerate NAD+ 
		- Reversible reaction
- Regulation of glycolysis
	- Enzymes ![[Pasted image 20260204104223.png]]
		- Hexokinase - allosterically inhibited by G-6-P
		- PFK 
		- Pyruvate kinase - inhibited by high ATP and activated by FBP
	- High ATP conc inhibit enzyme activity - eg PFK 
	- Intermediate substrates (AMP) stimulate PFK activity 
	- Inhibited by high citric acid conc and low pH
