## Anterior arm ![[Pasted image 20260204090959.png]]
- Muscle - BBC
	- Biceps 
		- Attachments
			- Short head - inserts onto the coracoid process
			- Long head - through glenohumerus capsule onto supraglenoid tubercle
			- Bottom onto radial tuberosity 
			- Also has aponeurosis 
				- Inserts into deep fascia of medial forearm - stuff attaches to it
		- Function
			- Attached to the radius -> Supination first, then flexion of forearm 
			- Very strong supinator - "screwing" clockwise motion
	- Coracobrachialis 
		- Attachments 
			- Coracoid process and medial surface of humerus
			- Quite difficult to diferentiate from short head of biceps 
		- Function
			- Mostly stability of GH joint (can have flexion and adduction at GH joint, but meh)  
		- Pierced by musculocutaneous nerve 
	- Brachialis 
		- Attachements
			- Attached onto coronoid process of ulna and anterior surface of humerus
		- Function
			- Prime mover in elbow flexion, especially when arm is prone
			- 
- Nerve ![[Pasted image 20260204091523.png]]
	- Musculocutaneous nerve 
		- Becomes lateral cutaneous nerve of the forearm - so no muscles 
		- Brachialis only C5-6? - yes but don't care. Musculocutaneous nerve is good enough
	- Biceps reflex
		- Deep tendon reflex - testing C5/6 spinal segments 
		- Elicits a flexion jerk

	- 3 layers - superficial, intermediate and deep
