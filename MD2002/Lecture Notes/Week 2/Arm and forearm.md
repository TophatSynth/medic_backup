# Arm
- 4 muscles 
- 2 fascial compartments 
- 3 large nerves
- Large medial neruovascular bundle
- Clinical - need to view as cross sectional medical image before operating

## Anterior arm ![[Pasted image 20260204090959.png]]
- Muscle - BBC
	- Biceps 
		- Attachments
			- Short head - inserts onto the coracoid process
			- Long head - through glenohumerus capsule onto supraglenoid tubercle
			- Bottom onto radial tuberosity 
			- Also has bicipital aponeurosis 
				- Inserts into deep fascia of medial forearm - stuff attaches to it
		- Function
			- Attached to the radius -> Supination first, then flexion of forearm 
			- Very strong supinator - "screwing" clockwise motion
	- Coracobrachialis 
		- Attachments 
			- Coracoid process and medial surface of humerus
			- Quite difficult to diferentiate from short head of biceps 
		- Function
			- Mostly stability of GH joint (can have flexion and adduction at GH joint, but meh)  
		- Pierced by musculocutaneous nerve 
	- Brachialis 
		- Attachments
			- Attached onto coronoid process of ulna and anterior surface of humerus
		- Function
			- Prime mover in elbow flexion, especially when arm is prone
- Nerve ![[Pasted image 20260204091523.png]]
	- Musculocutaneous nerve 
		- Lies between biceps and brachialis 
		- Becomes lateral cutaneous nerve of the forearm after it finishes the muscular part 
		- Brachialis only C5-6? - yes but don't care. Musculocutaneous nerve is good enough
	- Biceps reflex 
		- Deep tendon reflex - testing C5 spinal segments 
		- Elicits a flexion jerk at the elbow

## Posterior arm 
- Muscle ![[Pasted image 20260204091754.png]]
	- Triceps - "three heads"
		- Long head - infraglenoid tubercle
		- Lateral head - post. surface of humerus (sup. to spiral groove)
		- Medial head - post. surface of humerus (inf. to spiral groove)
		- Attaches distally to olecranon process  
		- Extension (long head - stability)
	- Anconeus 
		- ┐(ツ)┌  silly little muscle
- Nerves ![[Pasted image 20260204092103.png]]
	- Radial nerve 
		- Gives branches to long and lateral heads of triceps, then enters spiral groove (radial groove!)
			- Fractured humeral shaft -> tends not to affect extension of the arm bc branches before then (can still affect forearm extension)
	- Triceps reflex   
		- Tests C7 spinal segment - extension jerk

## Anterior forearm/flexor forearm
- Overview
	- Common flexor origin - median epicondyle of the humerus 
	- Common innervation - median nerve (except some)
	- Common function - flexors and pronators 
	- 3 layers - superficial, intermediate and deep 
- Superficial layer ![[Pasted image 20260204093344.png]]
	- Pronator teres (C6/7) 
		- Attached to radius
		- Has 2 heads, one to humerus, one to ulna - median nerve passes between
		- Pronator teres syndrome - gets inflammed, impinges on the median nerve
	- Flexor carpi radialis (C6/7)
		- Flexes the wrist, is on the radial side ┐(ツ)┌
		- Attached to carpals/metacarpals
	- Palmaris longus (C7/8) 
		- Attaches onto palmar aponeurosis
		- Goes into the hand - vestigial (absent in 15% of ppl)
	- Flexor carpi ulnaris (C7/8)
		- Have a wiiiiild fuckin' guess
		- Attached to carpals/metacarpals
		- Has 2 heads, one to humerus, one to ulna
		- Innervated by ulnar nerve, which also passes bt the 2 heads into the anterior forearm
- Intermediate layer ![[Pasted image 20260204093606.png]]
	- Flexor digitorum superficialis (C7-T1) 
		- Flexes the digits - attaches onto middle phalanges (except thumb)
		- Attaches onto median epicondyle AND radius/ulna/interosseous membrane
		- Tendon passes through carpal tunnel
- Deep layer ![[Pasted image 20260204171631.png]]
	- Flexor digitorum profundus 
		- Different innervations
			- Medial/ulnar half - Ulnar Nerve (C8,T1)
			- Lat. half – Ant. interosseous N. (C8,T1) - which is a branch of the median nerve
			- Able to test for damage of ulnar/ant. interosseous nerve by which distal phalanges you can flex
		- Attaches onto the distal phalanges, also passes through carpal tunnel
			- Has to pass through the tendons of the flexor digitorum superficialis
	- Flexor policis longus
		- Attaches onto radius and distal phalanges of thumb
		- Anterior interosseous nerve (C8-T1)
		- Flexes your thumb ✨
	- Pronator quadratus
		- Fraser's 6th favourite muscle 😌
		- Flat square muscle extending between radius and ulna 
		- Pronates the forearm weeeeee
		- Anterior interosseous nerve (C8-T1)

## Carpal tunnel  
- Contents  
	- Flexor digitorum superficialis
	- Flexor digitorum profundus
	- Flexor policis longus 
	- Median nerve - gets compressed during carpal tunnel syndrome
		- Supplies sensory for the lateral 3.5 digits and palm of the hand 
		- BUT there is a superficial cutaneous branch for the palm
		- So carpal tunnel syndrome doesn't affect the palm of the hand 


## Dorsal forearm 
- Common extensor origin - lateral epicondyle
- Common innervation - radial nerve 
- 3 grps - superficial, outcropping and deep
	- Except brachioradialis 
		- Elbow flexor, + 1/2 pronator and 1/2 supinator 
		- Attached to lateral supraepicondylar ridge and radius
		- Radial nerve (C5-7)
		- "Supinator reflex"
			- Tests C6 spinal segments - flexion jerk
- Superficial - Radial nerve (6-7) ![[Pasted image 20260204174207.png]]
	- Extensor carpi radialis longus
	- Extensor carpi radialis brevis 
	- Extensor digitorum 
		- All fingers except thumb
		- Only one - only need one 👍
	- Extensor digiti minimi 
		- For the pinky ┐(ツ)┌
	- Extensor carpi ulnaris 
- Outcropping - starts deeper, but then comes up over extensor carpi radialis tendons - all thumb stuff ![[Pasted image 20260205164049.png]]
	- Post. interosseous nerve (C7-8) - after radial nerve pierces supinator 
	- Abductor policis longus
		- Attaches to metacarpal 
	- Extensor policis brevis
		- Attaches to proximal phalanges
	- Extensor policis longus
		- Attaches to distal phalanges
	- Anatomical snuffbox - between extensor policis brevis and longus 
		- Crossed by cephalic vein and dorsal cutaneous branch of radial nerve 
		- Contains radial artery
		- Floor - scaphoid, trapeaium
- Deep - deep radial nerve (C7-8)
	- Supinator 
		- Attaches from ulna onto radius
	- Extensor indicis 
		- Extensor for index finger
		- Post.interosseous nerve C7-8

## Neurovascular relations ![[Pasted image 20260204095533.png]]![[Pasted image 20260204095536.png]]
- Median nerve usually runs between flexor digitorum superficialis and profundus
	- Then appears lateral to flexor digitorum superficialis at the wrist, has a superficial branch that goes over the carpal tunnel and a deep branch going through it
	- ^ self harm cutting -> very liable to be damaged bc quite superficial
- Ulnar nerve runs with ulnar artery
	- Both lie under flexor carpi ulnaris
- Interosseus arteries
	- Lie directly on top of the interosseous membrane, deep to everything
	- Post. interosseous artery connects to dorsal carpal arch
- Sensory supply ![[Pasted image 20260204095551.png]]
	- Medial cutaneous nerve of forearm - from median cord 
	- Posterior cutaneous nerve of forearm - from radial nerve
	- Hand 
		- Palm - Medial 1.5 digits supplied by palmar cutaneous branch of ulnar nerve, lateral 3.5 digits supplied by palmar cutaneous branch of median nerve
		- Dorsum - Same except lateral 3.5 digits (except nailbeds) are superficial branch of radial nerve
