# Metabolism
- 2 types
	- Catabolism - degradation of molecules to release energy
	- Anabolism - synthesis of new molecules to store energy

- 2 nutritional states
	- Absorptive (fed) - energy stored 
	- Postabsorptive (fasting) - energy released

		- Flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD)

# ✨ Energy stuff ✨
- 1st law of thermodynamics - the total energy of a closed system is constant, can't be created or destroyed, conv from one form to another 
- Activation energy - Gibbs free energy of activation
- Energy in biological systems is measured using Gibbs Free Energy (... _**sigh**_) ![[Pasted image 20260202123626.png]]
	- Exergonic - releases more energy than inputed
	- Endergonic - requires more energy than released
- Coupled reactions
	- 1st step of glycolysis ![[Pasted image 20260202124035.png]]
	- "Locking" glucose inside the cell - coupled to hydrolysis of ATP, bc the phosphorylation requires energy - but with hydrolysis, it becomes exergonic
