#anki 

Gene - length of DNA which encodes a particular protein
Chromosome - linear DNA molecule 
Locus - position of a gene along chromosome
Allele - alt forms of a gene 
- Each chromosome has one allele, but 2 chromosomes -> 2 alleles 
Autosomal - non-sex chromosomes 
- 22 autosomal pairs and 1 sex pair in humans
	- XX in women, XY in men
	- Y is the smaller one
Karyotype - set of all chromosomes 
Genetic disease - genetic change, not env, causing disease 
- Chromosome aneuploidies - diff numbers of chromosomes
Mutation sites ![[Pasted image 20260401151349.png]]
- Promotor and splice site - stop transcription/abnormal splicing 
- Base changes causing amino acid change -> protein change
- Base changes causing premature stop codon 
- Frame - the set of 3 amino bases -> amino acids - out of frame -> cause the entire sequence to be different when decoded - in frame -> just missing/added a new amino acid

Types of mutation ![[Pasted image 20260401151724.png]]
- Missense - change at one point
- Insertion - insert 
- Deletion - in frame or out of frame
- Triplet expansion 

# Mendelian inheritance
- Types
	- Autosomal dominant
	- Autosomal recessive
	- X linked
		- Y chromosome is almost irrelevant in terms of disease genes bc not many genes 
		- Eg [[Cell Adhesion and Extracellular Matrix#Disease in the ECM|Duchenne muscular dystrophy]]  
	- Mitochondrial 
		-  Many mitochondria -> many copies in a cell 
		- Important genes for mitochondrial metabolic pathways and ribosomal RNA's
		- Inherited almost exclusively from the mother, but sons and daughters equally affected 
		- Usually point mutations/deletions 
- Pedigree drawing ![[Pasted image 20260401153424.png]]
- Allelic heterogeneity - different mutations in the same gene can cause the same disease 
	- Eg hereditary haemorrhagic telangiectasia (HHT1 and HHT2) 
	- Can be a point mutation on endoglin gene or ALK1 gene
- Locus heterogeneity - same disease caused by mutation in one of several genes 
- Complications to basic pedigree patterns
	- Nonpenetrance - failure of genotype to manifest 
	- Variable expression of features of a disorder
	- ^^ Both seen more often in dominant conditions
	- Happens due to influence of other genes + env + chance
