Nerves
- Common fibular nerve ![[Pasted image 20260326151658.png]]![[Pasted image 20260326151745.png]]
	- Passes fibularis longus, around the neck of tibia - divides into superficial and deep branches 
		- Superficial - pierces deep crural fascia laterally – divides into medial and intermedial dorsal cutaneous nerve
			- Sensory innervation to the dorsal surface except the space between the 1st and 2nd digits
		- Deep – anterior muscles – under extensor retinaculum
			- innervates extensor digitorum brevis and extensor hallucis brevis
			- Sensory innervation to the space between the 1st and 2nd digits

Arches of the foot ![[Pasted image 20260326152621.png]]
- Weight distribution, react to uneven surfaces
- Formed by bones but supported by ligaments and tendons
- Each has a keystone wedge bone - required to keep the whole arch up
- 3 plantar arches ![[Pasted image 20260326152708.png]]
	- Medial longitudinal arch
		- Bones - Calcaneus, talus, navicular, the cuneiforms and the medial 3 metatarsals
		- Keystone - talus
		- Ligaments - calcaneonavicular plantar ligament (spring ligament), deltoid ligament, plantar ligaments
	- Lateral longitudinal arch
		- Bones - Calcaneus, cuboid, 4th and 5th metatarsal
		- Keystone - cuboid
		- Ligaments - plantar ligaments
	- Transverse arch ![[Pasted image 20260326152711.png]]
		- The cuneiforms and cuboid
		- Keystone - intermediate cuneiform
		- Ligaments - intercuneiform ligaments
