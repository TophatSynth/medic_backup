# Thoracic outlet syndrome
- ![[Pasted image 20260304150158.png]]![[Pasted image 20260304150055.png]]
- Leads to pain, numbness and/or tingling of upper limb. May have neck pain and headache too
- May trigger ANS symptoms, eg cold hands, swelling or blanching
- Cervical ribs
