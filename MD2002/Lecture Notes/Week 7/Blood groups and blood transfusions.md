Red cell antigens 
- ABO and Rhesus systems most important

ABO 
- ![[Pasted image 20260319152314.png]]
- Basically everyone has genes coding for substance H (the base of the antigen), some ppl will have A/B genes to add on top of that![[Pasted image 20260319152328.png]]
- Some ppl will have naturally occuring anti-A/B antigen IgG antibodies -> gives you A/B/O blood types 


Rhesus 
- Antigens c, C, D, no D, e, E 
- Inherited as a triplet, eg cDe
- Rhesus negative means D negative
- No naturally occuring autoimmune antibodies, but can develop in response to pregnancy or transfusion
	- Haemolytic disease of the newborn 
	- Foetal red cells carrying paternal antigens transfer to maternal circulation from about 20 weeks of gestation - maternal body can produce IgG antibodies against it, cross the placenta -> anaemia, jaundice, brain damage, death
