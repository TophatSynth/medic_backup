TAKEAWAYS
- Homeostatic systems bring all things (apoptosis, damage, repair) back to normal levels 
- There is a low level turnover happening at all times bc regulation and activation is happening constantly 


Oxygen free radicals in mitochondria
- Can oxidise stuff (burning it) -> not good 

Lethal injury - irreversible -> necrosis 
- The injury usually damages groups of cells 
- Necrosis also usually damages multiple surrounding cells

Apoptosis 
- Fragmentation of the cell - including fragmenting DNA
- Inner leaflet of cell membrane swaps to outside -> phosphatidylserine on the outside!! -> makes cell membrane sticky
- Factors 
	- Extrinsic 
		- Death receptors - upregulated when cell is under distress
			- Receptor bound -> cascade of caspase proteins -> apoptosis
			- Anti-caspases - clinical target
		- T cells - cytotoxic
			- Injects stuff to activate the caspases ![[Pasted image 20260316113311.png]]
	- Intrinsic
		- Oxygen stress in the mitochondria - metabolic
			- Damaged mitochondrion/too much free oxygen radicals -> releases contents - activates caspases
		- DNA damage
			- p53 - transcriptional factor - stops cell proliferation, induces DNA repair, triggers apoptosis
			- BCL2 - B cell lymphoma proteins 
				- Regulatory protein of cell death - some versions induce apoptosis, some versions inhibit 
				- Abnormal expression causes slow growing but difficult to kill cancer
	- Control of apoptotic factors
		- Anti-caspases 
	- When apoptosis goes wrong
		- Autoimmune 
		- Cancer
		- Neurodegeneration
