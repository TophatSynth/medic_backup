# Thorax 
- Function 
	- Protection of heart, lungs/airways, also abdominal - liver/stomach/spleen/kidneys 
	- Helps with respiration - resists negative intrathoracic pressure and provides attachment for muscles 
	- Haematopoesis - one of the last bits of remaining RBC production sources in adults - most of it is the axial skeleton
- Rigid but compressible 
- Connects with neck/UL/abdomen
- Shape
	- Truncated cone - wider at the bottom than the top, wider transversely than sagittal ![[Pasted image 20260910111216.png|210]]
- Thoracic cage is basically rib cage 
	- Sternum, 12 pairs of ribs, costal cartilages, 12 thoracic vertebrae and intervertebral disks 
- Thoracic wall is thoracic cage + stuff outside 
	- Skin/subcut tissue, thoracic muscles/fascia, intercostal muscles, mammary glands/breast tissue
- Rib arrangement 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260910111404.png|223]]
	- Goes from above posteriorly to below anteriorly

## Ribs
- ![[Pasted image 20260910111836.png|152]]
- 12 pairs - numbered from top to bottom
- Each rib ends with and includes a costal cartilage 
- Classifications - not mutually exclusive
	- True/false/floating ribs
		- True ribs (1-7) - attach to sternum directly via own costal cartilages 
		- False ribs (8-10) - attach to sternum indirectly via cartilage of 7th rib 
		- Floating ribs (11-12) do not attach to sternum
	- Typical/atypical ribs 
		- Typical (2-10) - head w/ 2 ridges for demi facets, neck, tubercle, body/shaft, costal angle  (where the rib bends most), costal groove (inf med surface of the rib, carries/protects the intercostal vein/artery/nerve - van)![[Pasted image 20260910112103.png]]
			- Costal angle is a good landmark for separation between dorsal and ventral rami of the thoracic nerves
		- Atypical ribs
			- 1st rib 
				- Deep, nonpalpable 
				- Short and stubby
				- Scalene tubercle - scalenus anterior attachment #link ![[Pasted image 20260910112421.png|250]]
					- Anterior - groove for subclavian artery
					- Posterior - groove for subclavian vein
				- Only articulates w T1 
			- 11/12th rib
				- Shorter and pointy 
				- Don't usually articulate with transverse process 
				- Only articulate with T11/12 respectively - no ridges 
				- Rib 12 is related to the pleura

## Thoracic inlet 
- Also called thoracic outlet or sup thor aperture
- T1 vertebra, 1st ribs, costochondral cartilage and manubrium 
- Quite small inlet 
- Slopes ant/inf from T1 ![[Pasted image 20260910114458.png|288]]
- Apex of lung extends 2-3 cm above anterior part of 1st rib/clavicle 
- Lots of stuff passes through ![[Pasted image 20260910114516.png]]
	- Structures that pass between the thorax and the neck 
		- Blood vessels for the head and neck
		- Trachea, oesophagus 
		- Vagus/recurrent laryngeal nerves 
		- Phrenic nerves 
	- Structures that pass between the thorax and upper limb - lie directly on top of the first rib
		- Subclavian vein/artery
		- Inferior trunk of the brachial plexus 

### Thoracic outlet syndrome 
- Misnomer 
- Compression of lower branch of the brachial plexus/subclavian artery/vein 
	- Caused by extra cervical rib/trauma/muscle ![[Pasted image 20260910114627.png|227]]
- Symptoms 
	- Pain/parasthesia in the medial side of the hand and forearm (C8/T1)
	- Weak grip 
	- Discolouration/swelling in UL

# Breast 👍
- Mammary glands is basically modified sweat gland 
- Breast forms along milk lines ![[Pasted image 20260911170931.png]]
	- Can form anywhere along there
	- Supernumerary nipple - looks like a skin lesion along those lines
- Areola 
	- Have sebaceous glands concentrated around lumps - secrete sebaceous fluid for help when suckling
	- Visible section of lumps is called Montgomery tubercles
- Location
	- Base of it is located between 2-6th ribs 
	- From lateral border of sternum to mid-axillary line
	- BUT! May have some breast tissue extending into axilla - axillary process - important pathologically 
	- Functional part is bt deep and superficial fascia - but also has superficial and deep breast fascia layers covering it but bt the skin fascia
	- Potential space between deep skin fascia and deep fascia of breast - retromammary space - between pec major deep fascia and breast deep fascia 
		- Blood, air, pus etc can get in
		- Breast implants are here

## Structure ![[Pasted image 20260910120709.png|255]]
- 15-25 lobes comprised of lobules 
- Lobes connected to lactiferous ducts to lactiferous sinus - storage of a little bit of milk 
- All surrounded by fat and connective tissue
	- Suspensory ligaments of the breast (Cooper's ligament) - condensed connective tissue - support + connect deep fascia to dermis 
		- "Orange peel breast" - something taking up extra space in breast -> Coopers ligaments pulling on dermia -> orange peel appearance ![[Pasted image 20260910120921.png|142]]
		- May or may not be tumours, but defo check it out
- Retromammary space 
	- Layer of loose connective tissue between the deep layer of superficial fascia and the deep fascia
	- Provides some degree of movement over underlying structures
