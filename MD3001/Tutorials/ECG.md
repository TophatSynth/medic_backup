# Notes

Measures electrical activity, not mechanical activity

Lead II is usually larger - more in line with electrical axis 

QRS complex - R is the first positive deflection on the graph (Q trough is sometimes not visible)

1st degree heart attack - PR interval massively elongated - electrical signals slowed down from SA → AV node
Secondary heart block with 2 to 1 filtration - Every second P wave doesn't send electrical signals to rest of heart 
Secondary heart block with 3 to 1 filtration - Guess. 
Tertiary heart block - no connection bt atria and ventricles - both firing on their own pace
