Pain 
- Is there anything that happens at the same time as you get the pain? - eg heart racing, sweaty, sick

Breathlessness
- All the time or does it come and go? 
- For how long has it been like this?
- Breathless sitting normally? 
- How far can you walk before becoming breathless?
- Can you climb a flight of stairs without becoming breathless?
- How far could you walk before and when was the last time you could do that?
- Can you lie flat when you sleep? How many pillows do you sleep with at night? 
- Do you wake up gasping for breath at night? Does anything else happen at the same time? - eg pain, heart racing
- Do you have a cough? Coughing anything up? - eg blood, froth

Palpitations
- Does it last a while or just one thump?
- What does it feel like?
- How long does it last for?
- Can you tap out the rhythm for me? 
- Have you had it before? 
- How often does this occur? 
- Exacerbating/alleviating factors? 
- Is there anything that happens at the same time, before or after? 
- Has it caused you to collapse or become breathless? Is it painful?

Oedema 
- Do you get swelling in your ankles? One or both? 
- How far up does the swelling come?
- How long have you had swelling? 
- Is it there all the time? 
- Is it getting worse?
- Have you noticed any pain, redness? 
- Have you noticed anything else? - eg fatigue, breathlessness etc

Dizziness/syncope
- Do you ever feel dizzy or faint?
- Can you describe the feeling? 
- When does it happen? 
- Have you ever fainted? 
- Does anything happen at the same time?
