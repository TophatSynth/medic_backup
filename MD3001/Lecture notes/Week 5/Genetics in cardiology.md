Roughly half of ppl with down syndrome have congenital heart disease
- ASD/VSD/AV septal defect/PDA
- Down syndrome - Trisomy 21 - 3 copies of chromosome 21
	- May not be 3 complete copies - could be partial copies 
	- Some genes have a strong correlation to congenital heart diseases when triplicated 
	- DYRK1 - gene that is involved w a lot of congenital heart diseases - encodes a kinase - can block that kinase or gene for treatment

22q11.2 deletion syndrome 
- Chromosome 22, region q11.2 been deleted 
- 1 in 4000 ppl
- Mneumonic for presentation - Catch-22 
	- Cardiac abnormlities 
		- Usually Tetralogy of Fallot or VSD 
	- Abnormal faces 
	- Thymic aplasia
	- Cleft palate
	- Hypothyroidism

Long QT syndrome
- Genetic, autosomal dominant 
- Basically slower repolarisation 
- Usually loss of function mutations in channel proteins 
	- Loss of function in K channel
	- Gain of function in Na channel - remains open too long - still open during repolarisation 
- Locus heterogeneity - mutations in different genes causing same phenotype
- Allelic heterogeneity - different mutations in the same gene causing same phenotype
