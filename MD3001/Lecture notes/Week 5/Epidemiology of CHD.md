Epidemiology - understanding where and why diseases/conditions are prevalent and where they're spreading, and then controlling and treating them 

Sensitivity - ability of a test to detect all of the true positives
- Number of positives obtained by the test / total number of actual positives 
Specificity - ability to identify the number of true negatives
- Number of negatives obtained by the test / total number of actual negatives

PPV - positive predictive value 
- The number of true positives, out of total positives 
- Telling you how likely it is a positive is true
