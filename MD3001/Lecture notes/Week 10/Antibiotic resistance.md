Antimicrobial resistance - broader resistance term that includes viruses, fungi and parasites 

Intrinsic resistance - all strains of a species are always resistant anyway
Acquired resistance - developed resistance

Minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) - lowest conc of antibiotic that completely inhibits growth of bact 

Minimum bactericidal concentration (MBC) - minimum dose that KILLS (DEATH DEATH DEATH DEATH DEATH) ![[Pasted image 20261118101624.png]]

Breakpoint - MIC agreed to be the threshold for antibiotic resistance 

Intermediate resistance - MIC is close to the breakpoint, but treatment with increased dose is still effective

- Impermeability and efflux pumps ![[Pasted image 20261118102244.png]]
