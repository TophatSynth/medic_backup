ARTERIES GO AWAY FROM HEART
VEINS GO TO THE HEART 
FRASER THREAT OF DEATH

![[Pasted image 20260917150927.png|246]]
- Oxygenated blood from placenta enters at IVC 
- Lungs are nonfunctional, therefore we bypass from RA to LA via foramen ovale 
- Liver - metabolically hungry and _will_ eat all the goodies - bypass via ductus venosus
- 

Placenta 
- ![[Pasted image 20260917151510.png|355]]
- Oxygen, nutrients, CO2 and waste products diffuse across the placental barrier - no mixing of maternal and foetal blood
- Usually posterior-fundal 
- Umbilical cord 
	- Surrounded by foetal membrane, amnion, and contains Wharton's jelly 
	- Contains 2 umbilical arteries and 1 umbilical veins (named relative to coming to/from the foetal heart)
		- Therefore veins contain oxygenated and nutrient-rich blood
		- Umbilical vein divides into 2 branches 
			- Smaller branch to liver
			- Larger branch - ductus venosus - bypasses liver and drains into IVC 
				- 20-30% blood bypasses liver into ICV 
				- This blood is directed by valve of ICV to mostly flow through foramen ovale → LA - oxygenated blood gets pumped directly to body, not really mixing with deoxygenated blood from body (somehow) 
- Foramen ovale
	- ![[Pasted image 20260918164056.png|189]]![[Pasted image 20260918164159.png|277]]
	- The mismatch in heights of the foramen ovale and falx septi allows the septum to act as a oneway valve/flap 
	- Pressure is higher in right side of heart bc lungs are nonfunctional → allows blood to open flap in foramen ovale, but doesn't allow blood to flow through to the other side
- Ductus arteriosus 
	- 90% of blood that goes from RV to pulmonary trunk ends up going into ductus arteriosus into aorta ![[Pasted image 20260917152159.png|220]]
	- Connects to aorta _after_ sending off brachiocephalic, common carotid and subclavian arteries - gives brain best oxygenated and nutrient rich blood 
- Umbilical arteries connect to internal iliac arteries - some blood goes normally, some goes back up to placenta

Oxygenated 
- Comes in as about 80% oxy, mix with IVC blood (25% oxy) → 65% oxy. Moves through RA → 60% oxy (brain), mixes with deoxygenated blood → 50% (rest of body)

Post natal circulation
- First breath - massively decreases pulmonary vascular resistance
- Umbilical cord tied - increases systemic resistance 
	- Closes up the flap at the foramen ovale - over the next few months will undergo fibrosis → close completely 
	- Happens in 90% of people - 10% have a patent foramen ovale 
		- Usually asymptomatic, but may cause paradoxical emboli - venous clot ends up in brain instead of lungs because it skips through the foramen ovale
- Obliteration of ductus arteriosus - yes it is actually the medical term for it
	- Functional closure - first hour
		- Aorta is now pumping oxygenated blood through ductus arteriosus 
		- Oxygen is a vasoconstrictor - huh? yea
		- Causes smooth muscle constriction 
	- Structural closure - 1-4 months 
		- Thickening of tunica intima 
	- Patent ductus arteriosus 
		- Blood can flow back into pulmonary circulation → pulmonary hypertension, congestive cardiac failure 
		- Can be caused by higher prostaglandin levels - can be countered by NSAID administration postnatally 
- Obliteration of ductus venosus
	- Blood flow through umbilical vein stops → muscle wall of ductus venosus contracts → portal venous blood starts to flow through hepatic sinuses 
	- Functionally closes within 1-3 hours, obliterates and becomes ligamentum venosum 
- Obliteration of umbilical vessels 
	- Becomes round ligament of the liver 
	- Distal parts become medial umbilical ligaments within medial umbilical folds
