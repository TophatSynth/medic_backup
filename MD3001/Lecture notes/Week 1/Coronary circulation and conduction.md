# Circulation
Heart muscle 
- Striated but involuntary 
- Fibres are branched but join together with intercalated discs


Blood supply to heart
- Coronary arteries 
- Right and left coronary arteries + anastomoses bt 
	- But anastomoses aren't very good, so if arteries are blocked - those entire regions are going to be ischaemic
	- BUT if you have chronic low level ischaemia (eg angina) the anastomoses can grow stronger
- Right coronary artery 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260917113152.png|276]]
	- Origin is hidden behind pulmonary trunk, passes down anterior of heart, then to inferior surface
	- Branches
		- SA Nodal branch at the top
		- Right marginal branch just before it goes inferior
		- Goes inferior → branches into posterior interventricular artery (goes more inferior) and AV nodal branch (dives deep into the AV node)
	- View on angiogram - left anterior oblique view
- Left coronary artery (aka left main stem)
	- ![[Pasted image 20260917113143.png|235]]
	- Also called the widowmaker - supplies majority of the heart wall so if fails → kaput
	- Origin bt left auricle and pulmonary trunk, splits there into: 
		- Circumflex artery given off first, travels round the back, gives off left marignal branch
		- Anterior interventricular
	- View on angiogram - right anterior oblique view
- ![[Pasted image 20260917113232.png|288]]
- Coronary dominance
	- Where the posterior interventricular artery comes off of
	- Right coronary dominance - norm in 85% of ppl 
	- Left coronary dominance - posterior interventricular artery comes off the circumflex branch of the left coronary artery - 12% of ppl
	- 3% codominance or other 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260917113442.png]]![[Pasted image 20260917113454.png]]
- Obstructions 
	- Right coronary obstruction → inferior infarct + dysrythmia in mild cases
	- Left anterior descending obstruction → anterior infarc 
	- Circumflex obstruction → lateral infarc 
	- Septal infarcts cause loss of LV function → ventricular fibrillation
- Treatments
	- Angioplasty 
		- Balloon 
		- +/- Stent 
		- ![[Pasted image 20260917113857.png]]
	- CABG - Coronary artery bypass graft ![[Pasted image 20260917113913.png|211]]

Cardiac veins 
- Great cardiac vein following anterior interventricular artery - joins coronary sinus
- Small cardiac vein accompanies right marginal artery - into coronary sinus
- Middle cardiac vein follows posterior interventricular artery - drains into coronary sinus 
- Anterior cardiac veins - drain independently into the right atrium 
- Coronary sinus - lies between left atrium and ventricle 
	- Drains 60% of venous blood into right atrium 
	- 40% drained by thebesian veins (small veins in the heart walls that drain into their respective chambers) and anterior cardiac veins 


# Conduction 
![[Pasted image 20260917114952.png|148]]![[Pasted image 20260917115027.png|339]]
- SA/AV nodes are groups of specialised modified cardiac muscle cells lying immediately beneath endocardium 
- Components of conduction system - SA node → AV node → bundle of His (actually bundles of Purkinje fibres - left and right bundle branches) → Purkinje fibres 
- SA node - anterior to opening of SVC 
- AV node - posterior-inferior part of interartrial septum, close to opening of the coronary sinus 
- Bundle of His
	- Right bundle
		- Right side of membranous part of IV septum
		- Travels through septomarginal band 
	- Left bundle 
		- Left side of membranous part of IV septum
- Nerve supply to the heart 
	- The medulla has cardioacceleratory and cardioinhibitory centres 
		- Presynaptic SNS fibres travel in T1-5 spinal nerves → paraverteral chain → SNS cardiac nerves - increase HR and force of contraction, dilate coronary arteries 
		- PSNS fibres travel in the vagus nerve → PSNS cardiac nerves - decrease heart rate, constrict coronary arteries 
	- Both supply the superficial and deep cardiac plexuses at the base of the heart
	- ![[Pasted image 20260918183717.png]]
- Cardiac referred pain
	- Pain fibres from the heart travel with nerves from T1-5 spinal nerves → referred pain to the T1-5 dermatomes - anterior chest wall and sometimes medial aspect of left arm
