Ultrasound to examine heart

Probe manipulation - PARRT
- ![[Pasted image 20260921102124.png|191]]
- Pressure 
- Alignment (movement)
	- Slide: Movement of the probe in the long axis along the surface of the body
	- Sweep – Movement of the probe in the short axis along the surface of the body
- Rotation
- Rocking: Movement of the probe along its long axis without changing the point of contact between the probe and the surface of the body
- Tilting (fanning)

Orientation marker must point towards top or right side of patient - except in echocardiography 
Top of monitor is superficial, bottom is deep 

Imaging windows - where to place probe
- ![[Pasted image 20260921102449.png|125]]
- Parasternal 
	- Parasternal long axis 
		- 3-4th left intercostal space, marker pointing towards patient's right shoulder ![[Pasted image 20260921102632.png|337]]
	- Parasternal short axis (mitral valve level)
		- 3-4th left intercostal space, marker pointing towards patient's left shoulder, perpendicular to chest wall![[Pasted image 20260921102820.png|341]]
	- Parasternal short axis (papillary muscle level)
		- 3-4th left intercostal space, marker pointing towards patient's left shoulder, tranducer tilted to point slightly downwards towards patient's left flank ![[Pasted image 20260921103122.png|356]]
	- Parasternal short axis (aortic valve level)
		- 3-4th left intercostal space, marker pointing towards patient's left shoulder, tranducer tilted to point slightly upwards towards patient's right shoulder ![[Pasted image 20260921103207.png|372]]
- Apical 
	- Apical four-chamber (A4C)
		- Placed on the apex of the heart, marker pointing at patient's left side, tilted up to look into heart ![[Pasted image 20260921103341.png]]
	- Apical five chamber (A5C)
		- Placed on the apex of the heart, marker pointing at patient's let side, tilt up further than A4C until the aortic valve appears ![[Pasted image 20260921103516.png]]
- Subcostal 
	- Subcostal four chamber 
		- Patient is supine, transducer placed 2-3cm below xyphoid process, pointing towards patient's left shoulder, marker pointing to 3oclock, pointing up into heart ![[Pasted image 20260921103702.png|340]]
	- Subcostal IVC 
		- Patient is supine, transducer placed 2-3cm below xyphoid process, marker pointing 90 degrees away from patient's left shoulder, pointing up into the heart

Echocardiography in resus (POCUS)
- Used to quickly rule out certain things 
	- Pericardial effusion
	- Enlarged RV/LV
	- LV systolic function
