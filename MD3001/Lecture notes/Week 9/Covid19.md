SARS-COV2
- Single stranded RNA VIRUS, coronavirus no 7 
- Lots of variants 
- Came from bats 🫰
- 4 major structures - Spike protein, membrane, envelope, nucleocapsid
- Binds to ACE2 in the lungs to get into cell → dissolves and then dumps viral RNA into cytoplasm ![[Pasted image 20261114114112.png]]
	- Or binds to TMPRSS2 → dumps viral RNA as part of fusing into cell membrane
- Variant of concern (VOC)
	- Many variants came about 
	- Natural "spontaneous" mutations would have occured
	- But also pressure from drugs therapies and more importantly chronic infections - virus enters, changes, leaves as a new strain

Clinical features
- History of fever 
- Cough, breathlessness, fatigue
- Lots of GI symptoms - diarrhoea, vomiting 
- Myalgia
- Bilateral crackles 

Risk factors - SO AFRAID
- Sex
- Obesity
- Age>60
- Frailty
- Respiratory disease
- cArdiovascular disease
- Immunosuppression
- Diabetes

Diagnostics
- PCR and Point Of Care Test (POCT) on top of clinical diagnosis - negative test did not mean no infection
	- Swab for test is operator dependent, or virus could have moved on from upper resp tract - problem now in lungs
- Antibody testing 
	- Not rlly helpful for acute covid as IgM takes about 10 days, and IgG is even slower (6-12 months)
- Imaging 
	- CXR - pneumonia, heart failure, early ARDS - normal CXR is reassuring

Mechanism of infection and disease
- 2 stages - viraemia (virus in blood), then inflammation cascade from that
- Antivirals useful in 1st stage, not so much stage 2

Acute vs postacute vs long covid 
- Acute lasts <4 weeks
- Post covid syndrome if it lasts >12 weeks

Management now
- Acute management 
	- Diagnosis 
		- Positive swab + clinical diagnosis
		- Then triage based on severity and high risk list - list is constantly updated by NICE 
	- Treatment for preventing severe disease
		- 1st line - Paxlovid (oral)
		- 2nd line - Sotrovimab (IV)
		- 3rd line - Remdesivir (IV, 3 day course)
		- 4th line - Molnupiravir (oral)
	- Treating acute severe disease - has covid and requiring oxygen
		- Dexamethasone and tocilizumab 
		- Can also give preventative medication
