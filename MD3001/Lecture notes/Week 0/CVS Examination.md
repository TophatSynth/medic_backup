Introduction and explanation.
- Position patient at 45 degrees w/ chest adequately exposed
Inspection.
- End of bed
- Capillary refill time 
- Central and peripheral cyanosis
- Malar flush - high colour over cheekbones w/ bluish tinge (low blood oxygen conc) - sign of mitral valve disease 
- Pale conjunctiva - anaemia ![[Pasted image 20260910161459.png|374]]
- Butterfly rash - rash on cheeks and across bridge of nose - systemic lupus erythematosus 
- ![[Pasted image 20260910161642.png]]
- Clubbing - changes in fingertips - low blood oxygen - lung cancer 
- Infective endocarditis - low grade intermittent fever, with heart murmurs
	- Roth spots - retinal hemorrhages with small clear centres 
	- Subungual hemorrhages - dark red linear lesions in the nail beds 
- Petechiae - rash that doesn't fade when pressed on
Palpation.
- Pulses 
	- Both radial pulses 
	- Rate and rhythm on right radial pulse 
		- Collapsing pulse - feels like it hits your hand - aortic regurgitation
		- Regularly irregular - ectopic 
		- Irregularly irregular - atrial fibrillation
	- Volume and character on right carotid pulse 
		- Low volume - hypovolemia, left ventricular failure 
		- Increased volume, anaemia, fever, thyrotoxicosis
		- Slow rising pulse - aortic stenosis
		- Collapsing pulse - aortic regurgitation
	- Jugular venous pulse (JVP) assessment
		- Patient resting at angle of 45 degrees, look for the JVP 
		- Closely reflects pressure changes within right atrium #tofinish 
		- ![[Pasted image 20260910163103.png]]
- BP
- Praecordium (the bit of chest over the heart) #tofinish 
	- Heaves - when heart beats, your arm physically lifts up
	- Murmur
		- Stenosis (tight valve) or leaky (floppy valve) 
		- Thrill - can feel murmur by palpation
		- Systolic - between lub and dub
		- Diastolic - after lubdub 
		- Patent - murmur goes through both systolic and diastolic
		- ![[Pasted image 20260910164355.png]]![[Pasted image 20260910163735.png]]
(Percussion).
Auscultation (stethoscope)
- Palpate carotid while listening, make sure heart and carotid pulse are at the same time 
- Distinguish 1st/2nd heart sounds
- Listen for heart sounds, added sounds, murmurs 
- Listen in all 4 key areas 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260910163812.png]]![[Pasted image 20260910163814.png]]
- Grades of murmur ![[Pasted image 20260910164116.png|379]]
- Maneuvers to accentuate murmurs 
	- Bell at apex in expiration in left lateral position. Accentuation of diastolic murmur of mitral stenosis.
	- At left axilla with diaphragm. Radiation of systolic murmur of mitral regurgitation.
	- At lower left sternal edge with patient sat forwards. With diaphragm in expiration. Accentuation of diastolic murmur of aortic regurgitation.
	- Over carotids. With diaphragm in held inspiration. Accentuation of murmur of aortic stenosis radiation / carotid bruits
