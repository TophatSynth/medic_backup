Ligaments 
- Extrinsic ligaments 
	- Thyrohyoid membrane
		- Med/lat thickenings - med/lat thyrohyoid ligaments
		- Has small aperture for superior laryngeal nerve/artery 
	- Hyoepiglottic ligament
	- Cricotracheal ligament 
- Intrinsic ligaments
	- ![[Pasted image 20261028164626.png|265]]
	- Collectively called the fibroelastic membrane
	- Subdivided into:
	- Quadrangular membrane 
		- Connects to lateral aspect of epiglottic cartilage, bit of thyroid cartilage, and arytenoid cartilage. Free inferior border with vestibular ligament (ligament running between thyroid and arytenoid) 
	- Conus elasticus
		- Mostly connects to cricoid cartilage, bit to the thyroid, free medial border with vocal ligament (between thyroid and arytenoid but below vestibular ligament)
	- Space between vocal and vestibular ligaments covered by mucosa ![[Pasted image 20261028165130.png]]
		- Aryepiglottic fold - Coming from external aspect, looping over quadrangular membrane superiorly 
		- Vestibular fold - covers vestibular ligament 
		- Laryngeal ventricle - bit that covers and loops between the space between vocal/vestibular ligaments 
		- Vocal fold - fuckin guess
		- Rima glotidis - space in the middle between the vocal folds
		- All mucosa is respiratory epithelium except vocal folds - stratified non-keratinising squamous epithelium 

Muscles 
- Extrinsic ![[Pasted image 20261028165726.png]]
	- Pharyngeal elevators
		- Stylopharyngeus - styloid process of skull to pharynx
		- Palatopharyngeus - soft palate to pharynx
		- Salpingopharyngeus - pharyngotympanic tube to pharynx 
	- Suprahyoid muscles ![[Pasted image 20261028170217.png]]
		- Digastric - "2 bellies" - ant/post belly 
		- Mylohyoid - thin flat diamond shaped one behind the others, forms floor of mouth
		- Stylohyoid - inferior part of tendon that attaches to hyoid bone bifurcates to let digastric tendon pass through 
		- Geniohyoid - anterior mandible to hyoid
		- All elevators of larynx (pulls hyoid up, pull larynx up)
	- Infrahyoid muscles
		- Sternohyoid
		- Thyrohyoid
		- Sternothyroid
		- Omohyoid - scapula to hyoid 
		- Depress larynx 
- Intrinsic ![[Pasted image 20261028174626.png]]![[Pasted image 20261030135124.png]]
	- Cricothyroid - rotates thyroid anteriorly - tenses vocal ligament → higher pitch
	- Thyroarytenoid - pulls arytenoids anteriorly - relaxes vocal ligament
		- Medial portions called vocalis muscles 
	- Posterior cricoarytenoid - rotates the arytenoids laterally - abducts vocal processes - widens the rima glottidis 
		- The only airway opener muscle - larynx is open at rest anyway 
	- Lateral cricoarytenoid - adducts vocal processes and rima glottidis
	- Transverse arytenoid - pulls the arytenoids together and fully closes rima glottidis 
	- Oblique arytenoid - pulls the epiglottis shut - "purse style action" - also closes off rima glottidis 
	- Innervated mostly by recurrent laryngeal nerves except cricothyroid muscle (innervated a branch of the superior laryngeal nerve)

Innervation 
- Vagus nerve :D ![[Pasted image 20261030135649.png]]
	- Superior laryneal 
		- Internal branch - sensory above vocal cord
		- External branch - motor for cricothyroid 
	- Recurrent laryngeal - motor for all other laryngeal muscles AND sensory to vocal cords and below 

Arterial supply ![[Pasted image 20261030135837.png]]
- Superior laryngeal arteries ← superior thyroid artery ← ECA
- Inferior laryngeal branch of inferior thyroid arteries ← thyrocervical trunk ← subclavian artery 

Venous drainage ![[Pasted image 20261030135848.png]]
- 3 routes
	- Superior laryngeal vein→ sup thyroid veins → IJV
	- Middle thyroid veins → IJV
	- Inferior laryngeal veins → inf thyroid veins → brachiocephalic veins

Lymphatics ![[Pasted image 20261030140121.png]]
- Above vocal folds → superior deep cervical nodes
- Below vocal folds → pre/paratracheal nodes → inferior deep cervical nodes 

Clinical considerations 
- Mostly just airway blockage via various means
- Choking
- Anaphylaxis
