# Bronchi
![[Pasted image 20261029111602.png]]


Primary bronchi (main) → secondary bronchi (lobar, 3 right, 2 left) → tertiary bronchi (segmental) → bronchioles (splits up to 20 times) → respiratory bronchioles → alveolar ducts, alveolar sacs 
- Trachea - cartilage
- Bronchioles - no cartilage, has smooth muscle
- Terminal bronchiole
	- Alternating thick and thin portions
	- Club cells - noncilliated cuboidal epithelium - produce protective secretions 
	- ![[Pasted image 20261029112606.png]]
- Alveoli 
	- Type I pneumocytes - gas exchange
	- Type II pneumocytes - produces surfactant

## Right lung 
![[Pasted image 20261029113844.png]]
![[Pasted image 20261029113847.png]]


Root of lung
- Contents
	- Main bronchus
	- Pulmonary artery (deoxygenated blood to lung)
	- Pulmonary veins (oxygenated blood to heart)
	- Bronchial arteries – supplying bronchi and structural tissue of lung
	- Bronchial veins
	- Lymph nodes and vessels 

Arterial supply - bronchial arteries
	- Left - 2 arteries from aorta 
	- Right - 1 artery from 3rd intercostal artery 

Venous drainage
- Bronchial veins - Right drains to azygos, left drains to hemiazygos, or may enter the pulmonary circulation back to the heart 
- Pulmonary veins - 2 per lung (sup/inf)

Innervation 
- Pulmonary plexus - Ant and post parts

Lymph ![[Pasted image 20261029114220.png]]
- Subpleural plexus → intrapulmonary nodes → hilar nodes → tracheobronchial nodes → paratracheal nodes → bronchomediastinal trunks
