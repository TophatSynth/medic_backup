Pulmonary ventilation 
- Getting air into respiratory system 

Alveolar ventilation 
- Getting air specifically into respiratory surfaces 

Ventilation rate 
- Pulmonary ventilation rate = Frequency of breathing * tidal volume 
	- Average person - 6000ml/min 
	- Aka minute ventilation rate 
	- Tidal volume is volume of breath at rest 
		- Normal is about 500ml
- Alveolar ventilation = Frequency of breathing * (tidal volume - anatomical dead space)
	- Normally about 4200ml/min
		- Still not completely "fresh" air as we don't completely empty lungs out
	- Anatomical dead space - anywhere there is no respiratory surface 
		- Normally about 150ml
	- Physiological deadspace - any areas where no gas exchange happens due to disease

Vital capacity 
- Volume = Full deep breath in - full deep breath out 

Static properties of lungs
- 4mmHg less pressure in pleural space than in atmosphere 
- ![[Pasted image 20261102131201.png]]
- Inspiration - pleural pressure drops instantaenously, pulls pleura out, decreases alveolar pressure more smoothly - alveolar pressure also equilibriates as air comes in
- Most energy for breathing is expended on inspiration - elastic recoil and gravity help with expiration

Dynamic lung mechanics
- Air flow = pressure change / airway resistance ![[Pasted image 20261102131507.png]]![[Pasted image 20261102131531.png]]
	- Airway resistance increases greatly with radius decrease - resistance should increase, but doesn't bc of sheer number of airways
	- Resistance increases for first few splits bc radius decreases, but then amount of airways increase exponentially, increasing total radius
- Compliance 
	- Static compliance (C) - lung expansion per increase in transpulmonary pressure
	- Changes in compliance in pathology
		- Emphysema - elastic tissues are degraded - less force to push against → easier inspiration
			- BUT also less force to push air out → less expiration
		- Pulmonary fibrosis 
			- Elastic properties are increased but compliance is decreased - more difficult inspiration, expiration is faster 
		- Both require active breathing → more energy needed + distinctive breathing pattern 
		- Chronic bronchitis 
			- Normal compliance but physiological dead space is greater due to excessive mucus 
- Elastic forces in alveoli 
	- Laplace's law → Pressure = 2 * Tension / Radius
	- If 2 alveoli are connected but have different diameters, air will flow from smaller avleoli into larger alveoli → reduction in surface area ![[Pasted image 20261102132724.png|185]]
	- Doesn't happen in health bc of surfactant ![[Pasted image 20261102132731.png|205]]
		- Surfactant reduces surface tension of water coating inside of alveoli - reduces force pulling them in - prevents collapse 
		- Surfactant producing cells only mature at 7-8months gestation - preterm babies are at more risk of collapsing lungs
