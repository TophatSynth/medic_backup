# Diaphragm 
![[Pasted image 20261031124230.png]]
- Attaches to margins of infeior thoracic aperture 
	- Xiphoid process 
	- Deep surfaces of ribs 7-12 and costal cartilages 
	- Right and left crura (singular crus) - lumbar attachment ![[Pasted image 20261031124446.png]]
		- Right is longer, connects to L1-3
		- Left connects L1-2 
		- Connects to body, anterior longitudinal ligament and IV discs 
	- Ligaments between attachments (vertebral body, transverse process, tip of 11/12th ribs) ![[Pasted image 20261031124758.png]]
		- Median arcuate ligament (in the middle, where aorta passes through)
		- Medial arcuate ligaments (between body and transverse process)
			- Psoas major passes posteriorly to it
		- Lateral arcuate ligaments (between transverse process and 12th rib) 
			- Quadratus lumborum passes posteriorly to it 
- Openings ![[Pasted image 20261031125006.png]]![[Pasted image 20261031125921.png]]
	- Caval opening (T8)
		- IVC and right phrenic nerve (+ some lymphatics)
		- Tunica adventitia connects with central tendon of diaphragm - keeps IVC open 
	- Oesophageal hiatus (T10)
		- Oesophagus, ant/post vagal trunks, oesophageal branches of left gastic vessels
		- Is within the fibres of the right crus 
	- Aortic hiatus (T12)
		- Aorta, thoracic duct, sometimes azygos/hemiazygos
		- Posterior to median arcuate ligament 
- Smaller structures ![[Pasted image 20261031125819.png]]
	- Left phrenic nerve - pierces left dome
	- SNS trunks - behind medial arcuate ligaments with psoas major 
	- Greater/lesser thoracic splanchnic nerves (least varies in path)
	- Intercostal nerves 
	- Anteriomedian gap
	- Sternocostal triangle  
		- Superior epigastric vessels pass through here
	- Musculophrenic vessels - pierces diaphragm anterolaterally around 9th CC
- Arterial supply 
	- Superior surface ![[Pasted image 20261031130117.png]]
		- Superior phrenic from thoracic aorta 
		- Musculophrenic and pericardiophrenic from internal thoracic artery
	- Inferior surface
		- Inferior phrenic from abdominal aorta
- Venous drainage
	- Mirrors arteries 
	- Sup/inf phrenic veins, some azygos/hemiazygos drainage
	- Inf phrenic vein also drains to suprarenal → renal vein 
- Innervation ![[Pasted image 20261031130459.png]]
	- Motor and central sensory - phrenic nerve (C3-5) 
	- Additional peripheral sensory from intercostal nerves (T5-11) and subcostal nerve (T12)
- Mechanics of breathing ![[Pasted image 20261031130623.png]]![[Pasted image 20261031130735.png]]
	- At rest, inspiration/expiration is mostly from diaphragm 
	- External intercostals contribute during inspiration ![[Pasted image 20261031130834.png]]
		- Pulls ribs up and out 
	- Higher demand during excercise etc can involve accessory muscles - forced inspiration
		- Can fill some of the costodiaphragmatic recess with lung to increase volume - can't get to 100% though 
		- Can also increase force/speed of inspiration via accessory muscles that attach to ribcage ![[Pasted image 20261031131221.png]]
			- SCM, scalenes, serratus' (moving ribs)
			- Pec major/minor (inspiration)
			- Lat dorsi (inspiration/expiration)
			- Abdominal wall muscles (expiration) 


# Surface anatomy 
Lung outlines 
- Anterior ![[Pasted image 20261031131324.png]]
	- Apex - 3cm superior to mid clavicle 
	- Mostly follows parasternal line from 2-6th CC 
		- Lateral deviation in the line between 4-6th CC for left lung - heart 
	- Midclavicular line at 6th rib - outline of lung
		- At 8th rib - outline of parietal pleura
	- Midaxillary line at 8th rib - outline of lung
		- At 10th rib - outline of parietal pleura 
- Posterior ![[Pasted image 20261031131649.png]]
	- Mostly follows paravertebral line
	- Level of T10 - bottom outline of lung 
	- Level of T12 - bottom outline of parietal pleura

Fissures ![[Pasted image 20261031131912.png]]
- Posteriorly T3/4, to 5th intercostal space in midaxillary line, to anteriorly following rib 6, terminating at junction bt 6th rib and CC
- Right lung - horizontal fissure meets oblique fissure in 5th ICS midaxillary line, then moves medially into 4th rib/ICS

Auscultation sites 
- Anterior![[Pasted image 20261102122741.png]]
	- Apex of lung - just superior to the clavicle 
	- Superior lobe of lung - anywhere above the 4th rib
	- Middle lobe of lung - 4/5th ICS 
	- Inferior lobe of lung - inf to 6th rib
- Posterior ![[Pasted image 20261102122851.png]]
	- Apex - above the scapula 
	- Superior lobe - above T3/4
	- Inferior lobe - below T3/4

 Lung recesses
 - Costodiaphragmatic recess ![[Pasted image 20261102123050.png]]
	 - Drain the recess - needle in 9th ICS angled upwards, careful don't go too deep
 - Costomediastinal 

Pneumothorax 
- Lungs held to thoracic wall by surface tension of pleural fluid 
- Air gets into pleural space → loss of surface tension → collapsed lung 
- ![[Pasted image 20261102123719.png]]
- Tension pneumothorax
	- Air is unable to get out of thorax → pressure building and pushing on lungs and heart 
	- Pushes mediastinum to opposite side
	- ![[Pasted image 20261102123907.png]]
- Chest drain 
	- Used to evacuate air/fluid etc out of the pleural space
	- Safety triangle - safe space to insert chest drain through ![[Pasted image 20261102124002.png]]
		- Borders - axillary folds
			- Lateral border of pec major
			- Anterior border of lat dorsi
			- Level of the nipple or 5th ICS
		- DON'T GO TOO LOW - danger of hitting liver 

Hemiparesis of diaphragm 
- Mostly idiopathic, can be iatrogenic or etc
- Diaphragm sits higher on affected side bc of loss of tone 
- May be able to compensate, depends 

Flail chest 
- Major trauma → >3 ribs fractured/broken → portion of thoracic wall completely separated from surrounding tissue → that portion moves inwards during inspiration ("paradoxical" chest movement) ![[Pasted image 20261102124358.png|248]]
