Remember to look for horses, not zebras, BUT! Always keep in mind that there may be something else underneath 

Hypersensitivity - collateral damage of the body by the immune system 
- Type 1 - allergy eg pollen 
- Type 2 - antibodies attacking complement
- Type 3 - immune complexes building up
- Type 4 - T cells damaging things 

Amyloidosis
- Abnormal deposition of amyloid proteins in tissues and organs 
- Amyloid proteins - can't be broken down by the body 

A number of cancer chemotherapies are quite damaging to the heart 

Diabetes 
- High blood sugar content causes microvascular damage
	- → loss of circulation to smaller tissues 
		- → neuropathy
		- → damage to vasa vasorum → loss of circulation to blood vessels → atherosclerosis 
		- → microdamage and ischaemia in the heart
	- Also causes glycation of proteins (maillard reaction!) 
		- Glycosylated haemoglobin - tells you the average of the blood glucose over the last 100 days or so 

Acute kidney damage → high blood potassium → arrhythmia 

Emphysema 
- Destruction of alveolar walls → loss of lung elasticity and impaired gas exchange 
- Main component of COPD
