33% of ppl worldwide are hypertensive 🫰

Pulmonary hypertension 
- Increased bp in arteries of the lungs
- Cause - usually not known 
- Quite rare, but more common as a comorbidity of another heart/lung condition 
- Usually only diagnosed when severe and symptomatic

Systemic arterial hypertension 
- _Persistent_ nonphysiologic elevation of systemic bp 
	- Systolic > 140 mmHg
	- Diastolic > 90mmHg 
- One of the major causal risk factors for CVD's 
- Risk factors for hypertension
	- ✨ complex ✨ phenotype
	- More prevalent in males under 60, in females over 60 
	- African americans disproporitionately affected
- Measurement 
	- All adults >40yo should have BP measured regularly, earlier if family history of atherosclerosis 
		- 3-4 pairs of readings over 3-4 months 
	- Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM)
		- Measured over the course of the day 
		- Measured twice per hour during waking hours, at least 14 measurements 
	- Home blood pressure #tofinish 
- Classifications 
	- ![[Pasted image 20261005091939.png|240]]
	- Stage 1 
		- 140/90 or higher, AND sbusequent ABPM/HBPM average is 135/85 or higher
	- Stage 2
		- 160/100 or higher, AND subsequent ABPM/HBPM average is 150/95 or higher
	- Severe 
		- Systolic >= 180, or diastolic >= 120

Primary hypertension 
- Aka essential/idiopathic hypertension #ask idiopathic?
- ~95% of hypertension cases
- No apparent single underlying cause ┐(ツ)┌ - combination of multiple things
	- Weight, lifestyle (diet, exercise, alcohol, smoking etc), genetic factors 
- Contributors to systemic hypertension
	- Increased SNS activity/sensitivity 
		- Isoprenaline (ISO) - synthetic β agonist

Blood pressure control 
- Short term
	- Cardiac output
	- Total peripheral resistance 
- Long term
	- Effective circulating volume
