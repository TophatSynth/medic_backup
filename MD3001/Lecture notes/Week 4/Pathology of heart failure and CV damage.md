Heart failure is not a single disease - it is a range of things contributing to a common outcome 

Beta natriuretic peptide (BNP) measurement 
- Released by heart in stress - good measurement but not diagnostic 
- Breaks down into NT-proBNP ← can also be measured

Myocarditis 
- Presents with fever and heart problem
- Damaged conduction → arrhythmia 
- Damaged myocytes → decreased ability to contract 

Preserved EF does not mean a normal heart 
- Amyloid deposits - reduces elastic ability of the heart → cannot refill enough but can contract so EF is preserved 

Non-pitting oedema
- Eg from hypothyroidism - extra ECM → oedema that springs back - not true liver 

Decompensation - inability of body to compensate for either chronic or acute condition 

Neoplasia of the heart #tofinish
