# Indications 
- Hypertension 
- Post MI, haemodynamically stable patients 
- Renal complications of DM
- HF

# Dose
- Maintenance dose in HPT is 20mg once daily 
- Maintenance dose post MI is 10 mg once daily for 6 weeks
- HF - initially 2.5 mg once daily, increased every 2 weeks by up to 10mg

# Contraindications
- ACEi and ARB's should not be used in pregnancy
- Angioedema or history of angioedema 
- 

# Side effects 
- Angioedema 
	- Allergic (histamine mediated) or non-allergic (bradykinin mediated) 
	- Non allergic usually occurs without urticaria and slower aonset 
	- Both have different treatment routines — follow local guidance
	- Discontinue ACEi's immediately if angioedema is suspected 
- Angina 
- Alopecia 
- Arrhythmias
- Cough 
- Dizziness 
- Palpitations

# Cautions 
- Diuretics
- Diabetes
- Patients of black African or African-Caribbean origin 
- Renal impairment
# Guideline
- Renal impairment - Start with low dose and adjust according to response
- HPT - first dose should preferably be given at bedtime
# Monitoring
- Renal function and electrolytes should be checked before starting, and monitored during treatmnet
