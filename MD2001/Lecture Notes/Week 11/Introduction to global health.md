Globalisation  
- The process of creating networks of connections among actors at intra/multi-continental distances, mediated through a variety of flows, including people, information and ideas, money, goods and services. 
- Has positive and negative impacts on health determinants
	- May make crucial services/resources cheaper, but exports may also drive prices up, or may not be sufficiently regulated

Global health
- An area for study, research, and practice that places a priority on improving health and achieving equity in health for all people worldwide
Vs public health
- Focuses on issues that affect the health of the population of a particular community/country

Disability adjusted life years (DALY's)
- One DALY = one lost year of "healthy" life
- DALY = years of life lost + years lost due to disability
- Years of life lost = mortality
	- Measures of deaths within a population or area
- Years lost due to disability = morbidity 
	- Measure of sickness or disease within a geographic area
- Global DALY's
	- Tell us where do most deaths/disabilities occur
	- Who is dying/disabled
	- Which conditions are causing the greatest loss of life and health
