## Calgary-Cambridge Framework
1. Initiating the session
	- Greet and introduce - name and "1st year medical student"
	- Use an appropriate opening question
2. Gathering information
	- Active listening
		- Asking open and closed questions
		- Summarising periodically
		- Ask about patient's beliefs
		- Agreeing on problems
	- Reflection
	- Empathy
3. Physical examination
	- If appropriate
	- CONSENT!!1!1!!
	- Explain what the examination will involve
4. Explanation and planning
	- Refer to patient's beliefs/understanding
	- Help the patient to take control of their illness management
		- Reasonable goals
		- Avoid jargon, split into chunks
	- Empathy 
	- Emphasise important points
5. Closing the session
	- Check understanding, clarify if necessary
	- Summarise
	- Safety netting 
	- Goodbye

- Verbal/non-verbal communication
- Build a relationship of trust
- Transition statements
