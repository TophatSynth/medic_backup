## Glossary 
- MSA – Mid Semester Assessment
- ESA – End of Semester Assessment
- MCQ – Multiple Choice Question
- SWA – Short Written Answer 
- OSCE – Objective Structured Clinical Examination 
- APE – Anatomy Practical Exam 
- Cut point – the ‘pass’ mark for an individual piece of assessment 
- Formative – practice, does not contribute to final mark 
- Summative – does contribute to final mark 
- Exam diet – period of assessment 
- 1 credit roughly equals 10 hours.

## Types of assessment
- Written
	- SWA only features in ESA
	- Questions relate to lecture material, guided study and practical class work
- Practical
	- APE - timed stations, identify structures/answer q's about 3D specimens
	- OSCE's - timed stations, perform skills, competency in clinical/communication skills

## Exam blueprint
- 20%  cell and molecular medicine (12 questions)
- 35% structure (21 questions)
- 20% mechanisms of disease (12 questions)
- 20% practice of medicine (12 questions)
- 5% clinical medicine (3 questions)
