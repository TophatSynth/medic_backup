## Moral theories
- Deontology
	- Duties/rules
	- Not concerned with the consequences - an action is right if it is in accord with a moral rule. The consequences don't matter
	- Moral rules are laid by God, reason, or by rational beings
	- Eg can't lie, even if the consequence may be dire
- Consequentialism
	- Consequences are what matters
	- An action is only right if it promotes the best consequences
	- How then do you evaluate which consequence is best morally?
- Virtue ethics
	- Act like a "virtuous" person would
	- A virtue is a characteristic a human being needs to flourish
		- Caring, disciplined, skilful, trustworthy
	- The idea that if you have good virtues, you will make good choices
