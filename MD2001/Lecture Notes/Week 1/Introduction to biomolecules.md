## Biological molecules

### Sugars
- [[Introduction to carbohydrates]]
- Have the general structure (CH2O)n
- Source of energy for the cell
- Can adopt different shapes and be joined tgth - monosaccharides to polysaccharides

### Fatty acids
- Saturated or unsaturated 
	- Changes the 3D structure of the molecule
	- Both hydrophobic and hydrophilic - amphipathic
- Can be stored as triacylglycerols/triglycerides
	- 1 glycerol, three fatty acids

### Steroids
- 4 fused carbon rings, differentiated by the functional groups attached

### Amino acids
- Amino group, side chain, carboxyl group
- ![[Pasted image 20250918103145.png]]
- Other uses:
	- As a src of energy in starvation conditions
	- Neurotransmitters
	- Precursors for other molecules

### Nucleotides
- Nitrogen ring (base) linked to a 5 carbon sugar linked to phosphate groups
- ![[Pasted image 20250918103438.png]]
- ^^ Nucleotide
- Nucleoside - doesn't have the phosphate groups
- Uses:
	- Short term energy store via phosphate groups
	- Store of electrons - NAD/NADP for ATP reduction in metabolism
	- Cofactors for enzymes - helper/additional molecule that allows enzyme to function; or signalling molecules
		- Can be used to stimulate enzymes to start
	- DNA
### Other notes
- Hybrids can exist - eg glycoproteins, glycolipids, lipoproteins
- 3D structure (conformation) very important
	- Structure determines function, subtle change can have a big difference 
	- Exploited by viruses and vaccines 
- Nothing exists in isolation
