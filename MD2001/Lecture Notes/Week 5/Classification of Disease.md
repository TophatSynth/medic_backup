## Diseases
- Not a box, but rather a cause, effect, wider consequences - we treat the patient, not just the "disease"
- Convey as much information as is possible
- Concept of wellbeing vs concept of disease
- Looking not just at the "disease" but at the root cause too

## How to classify?
- Pathology
- Aetiology
	- Always a combination of genetic and environmental factors
	- The patient's disease is always to some extent, "their disease"
- Pathogenesis
- Complications
	- Clinical, morphological, functional
- Prognosis
- Incidence - Number of new cases in a population over a defined time period
- Prevalence - Total number of cases in a population at a specific point in time
- 4P medicine - personalised, predictive, participatory, preventative
	- Key point is "patient centered"

## How to identify the cause
- How to distinguish correlation vs causation?
- The cause doesn't always result in the disease
- Identification
	- Probability of disease
	- Host predisposition
	- Cause versus agent
	- Causal associations
	- Koch's postulates
	- Exemplified by (thing)
- Idiopathic - "we don't know the cause"


## Why classify?
- To understand the disease and communicate information as best you can 
- Living with, not dying from
