Cardiac muscle
- Are branched and connected together by desmosomes at the junctions (intercalated disks)
- Each intercalated disk also contains gap junctions - electric impulse spreads through the entire muscle through the cells - when heart contracts, all the cells are involved 
	- Heart beating is dependent on electric impulse starting in one specific region then spreading out - Sinoatrial node (SAN) - intrinsic pacemaker
		- Intrinsically produces electric impulses, can be altered by P/SNS 
		- SAN has intrinsic bpm of 90bpm - PSNS is holding heart rate down at rest 
	- ~1% of cardiac fibres don't contract - they are more conductive than contractile 
- Striated, smaller fibres with individual nuclei
