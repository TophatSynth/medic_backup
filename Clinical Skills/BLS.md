## Chain of survival
1. Early recognition and prevention of cardiac arrest if possible (Unresponsive, abnormal/absent/slow and laboured breathing, short bit of seizure can occur at the start)
2. Prompt initiation of CPR (Call for help/999 on speaker and AED. Ambulance will give you codes for public access AED's)
	- Press on lower half of sternum, compress 5-6cm (~1/3 depth of chest)
	- Allow chest to recoil fully
	- Lower bed to knee height for chest compressions
	- Place dominant palm on chest, then other hand on top, interlock fingers. Shoulders directly above patient's chest
	- 30 compressions then 2 breaths
3. Early defibrillation
	- Attach electrode pads to bare chest (right shoulder, under left armpit), minimising interruptions to chest compressions
	- Ensure no jewellry, pacemaker, etc under the pad. Move up to 8cm away if so. 
	- If needed, dry and shave the patient's chest
	- Follow instructions, make sure nobody is touching the body or anything touching the body for analysing and shocking. Give loud verbal and physical prompting to stand back. Oxygen should be moved 1m away
	- Hold the AED and stand back, performing safety check around the patient. Never stand directly over the patient when delivering a shock.
	- Recommence CPR, 30-2. Breaths should be done with a bag valve mask connected to 15L of O2. 2 ppl technique. 
4. Handover and post resuscitation care (SBAR)

- Minimise interruptions at every point
- Perform CPR if you think they need it, even if it's on the street and/or a child. Good faith will rarely go punished. 


## Choking
1. Ask if choking
2. Encourage cough. If effective, encourage coughing and keep patient calm. 
3. If not effective, call for help and pull emergency buzzer if available. When help arrives, ask to call 999 for choking and to bring emergency equipment
4. Lean patient forward. Use non dominant hand to hold across chest, warn them you're going to commence back slaps, and use palm of dominant hand to hit between shoulder blades. Do this 5 times. 
5. Position behind the patient. Warn the patient that you're going to do abdominal thrusts. Make a fist and place the flat portion beside the thumb on the patient's umbilicus. Hold the fist with the other hand, and pull inwards and upwards sharply (like a "G"). Do this 5 times
6. Repeat 4 and 5 until either obstruction cleared, or patient falls unconscious. If they fall unconscious, perform CPR on them. 


## BLS
1. Check surroundings for danger
2. Shake and shout at patient
3. Call for help
4. Lie patient flat, open airway
5. Assess breathing and signs of life for up to 10 seconds
6. If help is available, ask to call 999 for an adult cardiac arrest and to get AED (or 2222)
