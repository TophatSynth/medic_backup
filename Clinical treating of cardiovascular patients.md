Hypercholesterolaemia - [[Cholesterol lowering drugs]]
- Statins
	- Blocks HMG CoA reductase
	- Used in diabetees, angina, post MI, prophylaxis of MI and CVA
	- Simvastatin 
- Fibrates 
	- Enhance lipoproteins -> increases conversion of VLDL to LDL
	- Also increases LDL uptake into the liver
	- Increases HDL production 
	- Fenofibrate, gemfibrozol
	- Used in patients with diabetes/bad blood cholesterol conc, or in patients with high CVD risk 
- PCSK9 inhibitors
	- Monoclonal antibodies that destroy PCSK9 - enzymes that destroy LDL receptors 
	- Alirocumab, evolocumab
	- Also Inclisiran! 
		- Small interfering RNA that turns off the gene that expresses PCSK9 - limits PCSK9 production

Hypertension - [[Pharmacological treatment of hypertension]]
- ABCD
	- ACE inhibitors/ARB's
		- ACE inhibitor - lisinopril
			- Side effects - cough, renal dysfunction, angioneurotic oedema
		- ARB's - losartan
			- Side effects - renal dysfunction but doesn't cause cough 
	- Beta blockers 
		- Cardioselective β blocker - only blocks β1 receptors - atenolol 
			- Used in angina, acute coronary syndrome, MI, hypertension and HF
		- Nonselective β blocker - blocks both β receptors - propanolol 
			- Same w above, but also in thyrotoxicosis and migraines
		- Side effects - can worsen HF in short term, lethargy, cold peripheries 
		- Can be given to ppl w mild asthma
	- Calcium channel blocker 
	- Diuretics (thiazide diuretic) 
- Mineralocorticoid antagonists
	- Blocks aldosterone receptors - spironolactone 
- Why start on calcium channel blockers if >55 years old? 

Angina 
- Different drugs for different purposes
	- Prolong survival
		- Aspirin, statins, ACE inhibitors, beta blockers 
	- Relieve symptoms - use, in order:
		1. Vasodilators - GTN, nicorandil, calcium antagonists
		2. Slow heart rate - beta blockers, calcium antagonists, ivabradine
		3. Metabolic modulator - ranolazine 
- Nitrates - GTN! Prodrug of NO 
	- Leave 8 hours per day nitrate free 
- Nicorandil - K ATP channel activator 
	- Side effects - mouth and GI ulcers

Atherosclerosis 


Acute coronary syndrome 
- Unstable angina, NSTEMI, or STEMI 

STEMI treatment
- Emergency - aspirin/clopidogrel (anticoagulants), angioplasty (if near hospital), thrombolysis (if far away)

NSTEMI treatment 
- Aspirin, angioplasty, statins, ACE inhibitor, beta blockers 

Antithrombotic drugs 
- Aspirin, clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor ![[Pasted image 20261014093215.png]]

Fibrinolytic drugs
- Streptokinase, tPA 
- Used in STEMI
